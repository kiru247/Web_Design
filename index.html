Welcome New Launch Sewri At Sewri, Mumbai

The New Launch in Sewri is a premium gated community on Zakaria Bunder Road, offering luxurious 3 & 4 BHK sea-facing residences with expansive decks and private elevators in select units. Designed by renowned SCDA Architects with Art Deco elegance, it overlooks the Arabian Sea and Mumbai skyline. Residents enjoy world-class amenities like a clubhouse, temperature-controlled pool, yoga deck, and co-working zone. With excellent connectivity via the Eastern Freeway, Atal Setu, and Eastern Express Highway, and proximity to top schools, hospitals, and malls, it defines luxury living in Sewri.
